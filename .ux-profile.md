# BrandOS

Audience: owner and small team selling on Wildberries and Ozon.
Primary job: see business health and turn financial/stock risks into assigned tasks.

Design system created manually from the brief; uploaded UI/UX skill has no search database.
- Base: #0b0e14; sidebar: #0e1119; surfaces: #131721 and #191e2b.
- Text: #f0f2fa; secondary: #a2aabc; borders: #2b3243.
- Primary: #8b7cf8; blue: #6d9fff; success: #58d6a3; warning: #f1bd6a; danger: #ff879a.
- Inter, 14px desktop body; 16px mobile body; 28px page heading; 28px KPI; 16px panel titles. Tabular numerals.
- 8px spatial rhythm, 4px internal micro spacing, 12px panel radius, no decorative gradients or blur.
- Page structure: operational overview with KPI row, two-column cash-flow/insights area and supporting tables; dedicated list, kanban and analytical views.
- Narrow 375px: collapsed nav, one column, inner table scroll, full-width drawers.
- Breakpoint tokens: narrow 375px / medium 768px / wide 1280px.
- Touch targets: 44px minimum; visible focus; reduced motion; WCAG 2.1 AA contrast target.
- Realistic marketplace data throughout, always explicitly identified as demo.
- No glass, rainbow CTAs, cartoon people, decorative input icons or static metadata pills.

Table updates (2026-10-20):
- All tables share data-table surfaces, 13px body / 12px header text and 12×16px cell padding.
- Unit economics and RNP use only source column names; source files do not supply values or formulas.
- Economics and RNP display all columns in a single horizontally scrollable table at every screen width. The first column is the seller article only and is sticky; product name is a separate scrolling column. A top slider and native scrollbar control horizontal position. Ten products per page.
- Sidebar navigation scroll persists in sessionStorage on reload and remains stable during rerenders.
- API values take priority. Customer data is separate; missing values stay unknown. No workbook sample rows or assumed tax rates.
- Verified layouts: 375, 768 and 1280px.

Economics column blocks:
- RNP preserves the original multi-level header hierarchy (product data, selling price, marketplace expenses with logistics/storage subheaders, revenue, taxes, results; per-model groups in comparison). Seller article comes first, then group and product; barcode columns are removed.
- Colored RNP blocks use distinct pastel fills with dark text; body cells match their block headings.
- Unit economics has seller article + the 19 source columns. Purchase and buyout are gray; price, ad rate, delivery, tax rate, commission rate and fulfillment are light gray. Advertising rubles, adjusted/reverse logistics, tax/commission rubles, defect/loss rate and amount, profit, margin and ROI are light purple.
- XLSX retains grouped headers, fills and frozen seller article. No workbook values or formulas are copied.

Compact values and covers:
- Numeric column widths follow the widest formatted value across the entire filtered result, with a readable minimum and cell padding; numeric values never wrap. Headings and text wrap. Widths remain stable across pages and update after font loading/resizing.
- In the table, the product-name cell becomes a 40×56 cover thumbnail in an 88px column. Name remains available in the tooltip, accessible button label, product card and XLSX. Seller article stays pinned.
- Covers use source-provided optional coverUrl (public HTTPS, no embedded credentials), never a URL guessed from article/SKU. Missing/broken covers show an icon with reserved dimensions.
